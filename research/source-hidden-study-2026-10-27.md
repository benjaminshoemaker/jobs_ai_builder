# Builder-job discovery comparison — October 27, 2026

## Question and unit

Can a qualifications-first, focus-aware search produce more worthwhile U.S. builder opportunities per review minute than the closest existing searches? The unit is a **candidate job posting**, not a company or an employer-board role count. Product-focused builders are the primary outcome; other builder focuses are recorded separately with an open vocabulary. User judgment, not a title or model score, is the relevance label.

## Collection completed

The [source-hidden pool](source-hidden-pool-2026-10-27.json) contains 58 candidate records gathered October 27 Pacific time from three routes: 18 from [HiringCafe's public U.S. AI Product Builder keyword results](https://hiringcafe.com/jobs/ai-product-builder), 22 from [AI Native Builder's AI Product Builder category](https://www.ai-native-builder.com/ai-product-builder-jobs) after its U.S. location metadata filter, and 18 round-robin title matches from seven manually selected public Ashby/Greenhouse employer boards. The [source key](source-hidden-key-2026-10-27.json) preserves route attribution separately from review cards. The 32 previously user-labeled reference postings were excluded where their exact URL or company/title matched. A company may still have different postings in the pool, and duplicate or changed-title listings may remain.

Selection is a **bounded convenience comparison**, not a probability sample of the U.S. job market. The two site routes use the page's displayed order and are a snapshot of only the collected slice; employer boards were manually selected after earlier research. The direct-board route therefore does **not** test discovery of unknown employers. A missing role in one route's collected slice does not show it is absent from that service's full inventory. Site listing freshness is unverified until the employer source is checked. Employer-feed presence means listed at collection time, not guaranteed open afterward. No authenticated LinkedIn pages were used.

The [first source-hidden review batch](source-hidden-review-batch-1-2026-10-27.md) includes 12 purposeful boundary cases, four from each route, shuffled and linked to employer postings. It is for refining the focus boundary and collecting reasons, **not** for estimating route precision. The [user labels](source-hidden-review-batch-1-labels-2026-10-27.json) are ten yes, one maybe, and one no. Product, operations, software engineering, and design appear; these are examples, not a closed taxonomy. TRM is a positive forward-deployed engineering builder because it combines mission-user discovery, personal software building, and feedback into the product roadmap. SevenAI remains maybe because the personal-building mandate is uncertain. These purposeful labels do not establish comparative relevance, review-time, or discovery lift. Compare route yield only on an appropriate held-out, source-blinded subset with the same verification standard and counted review effort.

## Early counterevidence and data-quality findings

- [HiringCafe](https://hiringcafe.com/jobs/ai-product-builder) already surfaces many of the user's earlier accepted roles; finding a role elsewhere is not itself a discovery advantage.
- The search-site summary for [ITS Logistics AI Builder](https://hiringcafe.com/job/ai-builder-liberty-reno-its-technologies-and-logistics-llc-reno-nevada-iu3tbk6oy5njr63h) described a prototype-and-handoff job. The [current employer posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/itslogisticsllc/jobs/5370389008) instead asks for production LLM/agent systems, enterprise-grade AI-assisted code, architecture and security judgment. Current employer text governs the review. This is one concrete summary discrepancy, not a measured error rate.
- [OpenSesame's current Greenhouse feed](https://boards.greenhouse.io/opensesame/jobs/8313424?gh_jid=8313424) reports a generic `Remote` location field while its posting body specifies onsite Austin. The body governs location. This is one concrete location discrepancy.
- The direct-board route yielded obvious adjacent roles (product marketing, applied science, conventional PM), showing that title/board retrieval alone still imposes review work.
