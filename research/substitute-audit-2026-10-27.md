# Closest-substitute and freshness audit — October 27, 2026

## Decision this informs

The discovery thesis is not that these jobs are unavailable elsewhere. The question is whether a qualifications-first, employer-checked, focus-aware view saves meaningful review effort or finds worthwhile roles a reasonable use of existing services misses. The evidence below supports a possible **triage and data-quality** advantage, but does not yet establish a discovery or time-saving advantage.

## Concrete freshness/metadata problems

The [OpenSesame employer posting](https://boards.greenhouse.io/opensesame/jobs/8313424?gh_jid=8313424) is a **part-time, 10–20-hour/week, onsite Austin** internship. Its Greenhouse location field says `Remote`, while the body explicitly says onsite. The [AI Native Builder role page](https://www.ai-native-builder.com/jobs/opensesame-ai-product-builder-intern-245d1b) displays an `Onsite` chip but also a `Full-time` chip, despite reproducing the part-time employer text. This is a verified, material job-type inconsistency, not an estimated site-wide error rate.

The predeclared twelve-role follow-up sample had eleven employer-current identities and one unresolved identity ([verification file](source-hidden-heldout-1-verification-2026-10-27.json)). Four of the eleven current postings had changed titles relative to collected source cards. This is a freshness workload for any aggregator, including this project; it does not by itself show that a competitor's current card is wrong. Granicus C04 remains unresolved rather than classified closed.

## Implication and next decision

Raw aggregation or a generic `AI product builder` query is a weak differentiation claim: existing services already find many strong matches. A narrower service could earn value by consistently separating **personal building from PM coordination or solutions delivery**, showing **work focus and engineering-career requirements separately**, and resolving **employer-body versus board-metadata conflicts**. These are testable advantages, not established product-market fit.

Do not ask the user to rate another broad batch. The next useful test is a short, same-task comparison: show what a user can find and verify in a fixed amount of active time with HiringCafe and AI Native Builder versus a qualifications-first view, using employer-current postings and recording duplicates, mistakes, and worthwhile roles missed. If the specialized view does not materially reduce review time or surface a few credible missed roles, use the existing services and stop product development on this thesis. The current fifteen-role cohort can seed that task without additional labeling.
