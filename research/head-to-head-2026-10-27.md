# Bounded builder-job search comparison — October 27, 2026

## Protocol fixed before new searches

**Task:** Find up to five current U.S. roles worth showing someone seeking AI-enabled personal building with product judgment. Other builder focuses count separately. A role requiring substantial professional software engineering may count as a builder but is not an ideal-audience match. Begin at qualifications, then check whether personal building and outcome ownership are central. Use the employer posting as the source of truth.

**Routes:** (1) HiringCafe's public U.S. `ai product builder` search; (2) AI Native Builder's public `AI Product Builder` category, filtered to U.S.-eligible workplace metadata; (3) independent public web search for employer-hosted postings, without seeding known company boards. For route 3, use these preselected queries: `"product manager" "ship code" "Claude Code" jobs`, `"AI builder" "operations" "Claude Code" jobs`, and `site:jobs.ashbyhq.com "product builder" "AI"`. No authenticated sites or API credentials.

**Rules:** Review in displayed relevance/order, skip the 44 already user-reviewed postings and obvious duplicates, and stop at five plausible current postings or a ten-minute active-work cap per route. Log reviewed cards, employer verification, likely focus, qualification gate, and uncertainty. A competitor-page miss is never a claim of inventory absence. Route 3 searches the web, not an implemented product, so this tests whether human/agent-led triage can add value, not whether our software already does. Because one researcher sees all routes in sequence and the samples are small, this is directional evidence only. Record actual active work time rather than inventing a matched duration.

## Results

This was a directional, one-researcher comparison, not a controlled speed test. The HiringCafe pass ran beyond its ten-minute cap while employer pages were checked, so the time cap was breached and no speed or yield-per-minute comparison is valid. Subsequent routes were scoped to the initial ordered U.S. slice and preselected queries. Discovery overlap and employer-page accuracy are still informative. None of the new roles below has a user fit judgment.

## Decision from this test

The central category is real, and existing public services already discover good examples. This test **does not establish unique discovery or faster review**, especially given the breached time cap, small sequential sample, overlapping sources, and no new user labels. It also does not establish that existing services give the emerging category a clear identity or serve as a trusted editorial home. HiringCafe's ability to return relevant search results is evidence about retrieval, not a test of the user's combined discovery and thought-leadership idea. The demonstrated curation gap is employer-confirmed current status, exact requirements, whether the person personally ships working software, the focus of the building, and whether a SWE career is required. A unique inventory advantage is not required to justify exploring a category-focused site. The next test should combine a clear public definition of the role, employer-checked examples, and useful writing with a concise collection of jobs. Judge it by audience understanding and engagement as well as discovery quality; decide what to automate after that value is clearer.
