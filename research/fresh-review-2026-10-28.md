## How to interpret the result

Compute inclusion precision and missed positives separately after user labels arrive; also score focus and SWE gate separately. An accurate list of mostly negatives is not enough to establish performance on rare positive builder roles. Keep unclear outcomes visible rather than treating them as errors or silently dropping them. This small watchlist batch cannot support a near-100% general accuracy claim. The labeling work established the rubric and calibration examples; it did not train or independently validate an automated classifier.

## Decisions API fit

OpenAI documents Decisions as typed predicate, choice, and score judgments. That suits separate builder-fit, personal-building, problem-ownership, delivery, AI-centrality, and SWE-gate questions. Use independent focus predicates plus an other/unclear outcome so new focuses remain possible. Its probabilities need empirical calibration; a high model confidence is not measured accuracy. Use Responses structured outputs when written rationale, exact evidence, salary extraction, or open-ended focus discovery is needed. Official guide: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions

A useful next comparison is Decisions versus a stronger structured-output classifier on the same unseen postings, with the rubric and examples fixed first. This batch tests current-agent judgment only.
